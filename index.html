Chopping Spree Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the Android game Chopping Spree (com.geminigames.choppingspree), made by GemIni Games ("we", "us").

Summary

Chopping Spree has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we run no servers for it. Your progress stays on your device. The one thing the game uses the internet for is buying the full game, which Google Play handles. We never sell your data.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress and your settings (such as volume, language and display options) in its own private storage on your device. This is only used to run the game. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the game does not back it up to the cloud.

Buying the full game

The game is free to start, and you can unlock the rest with one optional purchase through Google Play. Google takes the payment. We never receive your card, bank or other payment details.

To offer the purchase and to unlock what you have bought, the game asks Google Play on your device for the product's price and for your purchase record for this game (the product, whether it is paid for, and Google's order number and purchase token). The game uses this only to show the price, confirm the purchase with Google and unlock the full game. It does not save it or send it to us.

Google gives us sales records, such as order numbers, dates, prices and countries. We use them to support players, handle refunds, and meet our accounting and tax duties.

Google Play services in the game

The game includes Google's Play Billing library, the only third-party service in it. The library communicates with Google to make purchases work, and may send Google technical information about how it is working. Google handles this under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play also handles downloads, reviews and tester feedback. Google may show us information such as install counts, crash statistics, reviews and tester feedback. We use it only to improve and support the game.

Permissions

The game has permission to make Google Play purchases, to use the internet, and to see whether the device is online. Google's billing library needs these. The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Who data is shared with

Apart from Google, as described above, the game shares data with no one. It contains no advertising, analytics or social media services.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use them only to reply to you and to improve the game.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: progress and settings stay until you delete them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, removes them. We hold no copy, so we cannot recover them. A purchase is not lost: the game asks Google Play for it again.
  • Emails to us: we delete them if you ask.
  • Purchase records: Google keeps these with your Google account, and we keep sales records for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

Security

Saved data stays in the game's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Payments go through Google Play and never pass through us. Emails to us are kept in our email account.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has emailed us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email GemIni Games at inigeminigames@gmail.com.
